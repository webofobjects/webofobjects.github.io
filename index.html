💡 What if computers could organize information the way we think, not the way we file?

🧱 The software development stack is broken. Notion proved it. Vibe coding confirms it. People don’t want to build infrastructure. They just want to build applications.

🔒 This is not just a developer problem. Modern computing itself is too rigid. Every application invents its own way to represent data and traps it in files or databases. Closed ecosystems users don’t control, bound by rigid schemas and proprietary formats. As a result, information becomes fragmented, structure locks into place and stops evolving, and our ideas never truly connect.

🕸️ Web of Objects is a new computing layer that absorbs stack complexity, going beyond files and folders, beyond links and pages, to create a living network of interconnected objects that mirrors how humans think.

♾️ It offers a universal way to represent and connect information, structured or unstructured, personal or collective, within a single coherent environment.

🪐 This gives rise to a new space on the Internet, the webverse, where web pages become objects, websites become worlds, and the World Wide Web becomes a universe.

⚠️ But technology alone isn’t enough. The AI revolution shows that innovation can advance humanity while also displacing people. Not because technology is harmful, but because profit-driven systems use it to cut costs instead of lifting everyone.

🌍 We need a new way to think about the world. A web society that unites people across borders and cultures, where technology serves humanity and business is shaped for shared progress, not just profit.

🫶 Join our rebellion — because when humanity unites, the status quo doesn’t stand a chance.
